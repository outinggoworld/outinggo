The Anthurium Resort

A peaceful, serene resort near Wagholi, Pune — glamping pods, suites, villas and a full day of outdoor activities.

About The Anthurium

Anthurium is a peaceful and serene destination where visitors can escape the stresses of everyday life and reconnect with nature. The property offers various accommodation options — tented stays, standard rooms, premium suites and luxurious villas — along with great food, housekeeping and room service for a comfortable staycation.

Beyond the stay, guests can enjoy a full day-out experience with cricket, badminton, volleyball, mini golf, zipline and more, making it a popular pick for families, groups and corporate outings alike.
